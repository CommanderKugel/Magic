## Casting a Spell / Activating an Ability

9 Stepts from Comprehensive Rules of MTG.
Casting a spell is defined in the rules 601.2a-601.2i, which is 9 steps in total.
Activating an ability follows the same steps as casting a spell, (602.2).

1. Anounce casting a spell / activating am ability
   The spell is put from its current zone (hand, graveyard, etc.) onto the stack.
   Double sided-, mult- and split-cards decide what mode is played now.
   For activated abilities, the ability is placed on the stack as an own object instead.
2. Making Decisions

- modus from modal spells
- alternate/additional costs
- decide on variable costs (e.g. X in manacosts)
- hybrid-mana and phyrexian mana
- splice onto...

3. Choosing all Targets

* "target" from its effect
* for keywords e.g. "Aura"

4. Determine distribution

* "Deal 3 damage on up to 3 targets, ..."

5. Legality Check
   if 1.-4. made illegal choices until now, revert the boardstate until before the cast
6. Determine total cost
   total cost = printed-cost or alt-cost + additional-cost - cost-reduction
7. Use Mana abilities
   No non-mana abilities or spells can be cast now.
   Opponents cannot react to those mana abilities.
8. Pay the cost

- in any order, but one cost at a time
- partial payment are not allowerd

9. The spell is cast successfully

- Triggered abilities go on the stack
- Active Player receives Priority again
