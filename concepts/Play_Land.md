## Playing Lands

Lands are played. This is a special action, no spell is cast and lands are not spells.

0. Only allowed under special circumstances
   (305.2)
- it is a main phase
- the stack is empty
- the player has priority
- the player has played less lands this turn than they are allowed to

1. Announce playing a land
   (305.1)

2. Enter the battlefield
   (305.1)
- the land is instantly in play
- replacement effects ("this land enters tapped")

3. increment played lands counter
   (305.1)

4. triggered abilities
   (305.1)
- landfall
- "whenever a land enters the battlefield ..."

5. Active player keeps priority.
   (305.1)
- Opponents cannot react in response to a player playing lands.
