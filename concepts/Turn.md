## A Turn

A turn consists of 5 phases in this order - beginning, precombat main, combat, postcombat main, end (CR 500.1)

# Beginning phase (CR 502)
1. Untap step (502.)
- phasing
- day/night
- untap active players permanents
- no priority

2. Upkeep step (503.)
- triggered abilities
- instant speed priority (503.1)

3. Draw step (504.)
- active player draws a card (504.1)
- active Player gets priority (504.2)

# Main Phase

- Triggered abilities (beginning of main phase)
- Sagas (505.4)-
- active player gains priority (sorcery speed, 505.6)

# Combat Phase

only creature can attack or block (506.3)

1. Beginning of Combat (507.)
- active player gets priority (507.2)

2. Declare Attackers Step (508.)
- active player declares attacker (508.1)
    - must be untapped and must have haste or not be summoning sick (508.1a)
    - must choose to attack player or planeswalker definding player controlls (508.1b)
    - attacker checks restrictions (e.g. cant attack alone) (508.1c) -> maybe revert illegal state
    - requirements (e.g. attacks if able)
    - tap attacking creatures (not a cost) (508.1f)
    - determine extra cost to attack (508.1g & 508.1h)
    - activate mana abilities for costs (508.1i)
    - pay extra costs (508.1j)
    - attackers declared successfully. triggered abilities apply (508.1k & 508.1m)
- active player gains priority (508.2)
    - now "on attack" trigger miss the timing (508.2a)

3. Declare Blockers Step (509.)
- defending player declares blocker
    - choose which cretures will block which attacker (509.1a)
        - must be untapped
    - restictions (cant block) (509.1b)
    - determine cost to block (509.1d)
    - activate mana abilities (509.1e)
    - pay for cost (509.1f)
    - blocker are declared successfully´. triggered abilities apply (509.1g & 509.1i)
- active player gains priority (509.2)

4. Combat damage Step (510.)
there might be 2 if a creture has first-strike or double strike (510.4)
- attacking and defending player announce distribution of combat damage (510.1)
- attacking and blocking creatures assign combat damage equal to its power (510.1a)
    - 0 or less damage is counted as not dealing damage (510.1a)
    - unblocked creatures assign damage to player or planeswalker (510.1b)
    - blocked creatures assign damage to blocker (510.1c)
    - blocking creatures assign damage to creature they block (510.1d)
- combat damage is assigned simultaneously (510.2)
    - triggers (e.g. enrage)
    - state based actions
- active player gains priority (510.3)


5. End of Combat Step (511.)
- triggered abilities
- active player gains priority

# End Phase

1. End Step (513.)
- triggered abilities
- active player gains priority (513.1)

2. Cleanup Step  (514.)
- discard due to hand-size (514.1)
    - abilities due to discard (may) go on the stack
    - otherwise no priority (514.3)
- remove damage from all permanents & until-end-of-turn effects end (514.2)
